# Frogy v16.9 — Quest Movement Test Checklist

**Test the survivor first.** Record a short video for each failure; note whether the frog is grounded, airborne, or touching a structure.

1. **Stand normally:** Without crouching, lower hands to around waist height. Make short planted hand strokes. The frog should move forward/sideways only when the virtual reach meets the floor, not while swinging both hands near your face.
2. **Release and reset:** Lift hands away and swing them forward. The avatar should not snap backward.
3. **A jump and air test:** Press A. While airborne, wave/swipe hands without touching a real wall. The avatar should keep the prior velocity; hands must not add movement or an extra jump.
4. **Ground hop:** On flat ground, plant a lowered hand and press downward quickly. You should launch *somewhat* without A. Pressing A alone should launch higher.
5. **Wall and tree:** Touch an actual trunk/wall with a hand and push. The frog should move away from the obstacle; wall pushing can work in midair. Move around it without getting stuck.
6. **Same tongue, both hands:** Grapple with Y or B, release, immediately try the other hand. The tongue should remain unavailable for **7 seconds after release**. Retest when it has recharged; grappling into a blocked wall should release and start cooldown.
7. **Denser cover:** On each map, confirm you can reach trees, graves or props easily but that survivor spawns, weapons and medkits aren't buried.
8. **Preserved systems:** Test a weapon pickup/drop, the physical tutorial, Players Online on the Enter VR page, and Camera Lab from X → Settings.
9. **Mega Frog:** Only smoke-test that it can still jump and move. Do **not** redesign/tune monster speed until regular frog movement is approved.

If the frog feels stuck while standing, report headset model, approximate real hand height, floor-level room setup, whether a hand is shown as **SOLID CONTACT**, and whether A still works. Record this specifically so reach can be calibrated without restoring air propulsion.
