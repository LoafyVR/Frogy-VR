# Frogy v16.0: Actual Quest Controls

- Left X: VR menu (toggle)
- Right A: Jump
- Left Y: left-hand tongue grapple (survivors only)
- Right B: right-hand tongue grapple (survivors only)
- Monster Y or B: Roar (cooldown); monster cannot grapple
- Grip on each controller: grab/drop weapons or detached parts; monster left grip rips, right grip chomps
- Trigger: attack / shoot; hold on a wall to cling and climb
- Right stick: optional turning via settings

Avoid mapping X to jump: it is the menu button, and the owner has not approved replacing it.
