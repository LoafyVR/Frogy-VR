# Frogy v16.3 — Upright Movement Fix

- Standing-headset-height floor-assist now accepts normal lowered hands (not just crouched/knee-level hands).
- Downward arm presses can launch the frog at waist height with bounded impulse; applies to both normal frogs and Mega Frog.
- Forward, backward, and sideways strokes retain real controller displacement; tracked hands are never visually snapped to the floor.
- VR menu input does not accidentally propel the frog.
- Expanded automated headset-input simulation for standing push-off and monster parity.
- Full physical Meta Quest verification is still pending.

# FROGY v16.2 — Body Follow, Articulated Paws & First-Person Arms

## New in this update
- Frog-shaped 3-finger paw model with curls driven by controller pressure.
- Thumb curls on A/B (right) or X/Y (left); outer finger on grip; trigger finger on trigger.
- Two-joint arms inspired by Gorilla Tag proportions, attached to the body.
- First-person visible torso and arms, kept low to avoid blocking view.
- Subtle procedural body follow: delayed body yaw, mild up/down pitch, slight lean and crouch response.
- Natural blinking plus a very rare lip-lick idle animation.
- Slightly smaller, denser maps again for closer encounters.

## Still needs Quest playtesting
- How good the new arms feel in first person.
- Whether the finger curls match real controller pressure naturally.
- Whether the body visibility feels immersive without getting in the way.
- Final tuning of movement, especially tiny pushes and ground contact.
