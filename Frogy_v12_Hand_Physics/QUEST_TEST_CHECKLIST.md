# Frogy v12.0 — Quest playtest checklist

**First confirm the launcher says v12.0** at `https://frogy-vr.onrender.com/` inside Quest Browser. If it still says v11, inspect the GitHub commit, Render Root Directory and cached browser tab.

- [ ] Both paws stay aligned to the **real tracked controllers** while standing still, reaching down, jumping and turning—neither sinks 0.6 m below the hand.
- [ ] Only **one paw per hand** appears. The three fingers end in orange bulb pads similar to the supplied image.
- [ ] Alternating arm strokes push forward without needing to crouch or use joystick walking; a firm push downward can give a controlled upward push-off.
- [ ] Test against walls and **eight reachable swamp trees**: paws contact trunks, body cannot walk through trunks, downward hand strokes can climb.
- [ ] Jump onto each of the **seven lily-pad parkour pads** and stay on top after landing; try fast landings and pad edges.
- [ ] Press **right A** to jump: horizontal speed stays consistent. Press **right B** to tongue grapple. Hold triggers against walls for timed cling. Tap **left X** to open/close the menu.
- [ ] Lobby dressing mirror shows your selected color/hat/face item and the mirror frog does a **goofy independent dance** (bounce, head sway, alternating waving hands, happy expression). Moving your real hands must not move the mirror frog or your tracked paws.
- [ ] Other player's slimmer frog animates while moving and remains correctly visible with two hands and a readable nameplate.
- [ ] Map voting, private room codes, Crickets/Dabloons, cosmetics and menu still work. Test voice chat separately.
- [ ] In a full round, try walls, tree colliders and weapon interactions. Watch for stuttering or low frame rate.

**If something fails:** send Quest model, exact version label and a 20–30-second in-headset recording showing what the real hands are doing and what the game draws. Source tests cannot replace a real Quest playtest.
