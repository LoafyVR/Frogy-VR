# Frogy 19 — Five-minute Quest test

1. **Rotwood Swamp:** On a real Quest, move slowly and quickly between several trees. Try pushing with both hands and going around a shack. Compare with the **same movements on Gravebell Cemetery**. There should be no unexpected repeated world-position correction.
2. **Movement:** Test normal short/long pushes and a ground-powered jump with no buttons. The Frogy 17.2 push module was not edited; report any difference.
3. **Start:** After pressing the required **Enter VR** button to open the headset session, you should be able to move right away without closing an automatically opened tutorial/menu. You can still open the X menu when needed.
4. **Medkit:** At full 175 HP, try grabbing a medkit with grip (or trigger when unarmed and nearby); a visible VR alert should say you cannot use it, and the kit must remain in the world. Repeat with missing HP and check healing.
5. **Sound:** Approach a Mega Frog. Listen for a softer heartbeat approximately every two seconds rather than a loud repeated notification beep. Test normal shots, footsteps and melee at your usual headset volume.
6. **Monster bar:** Compare a two-player match (monster 300 HP) against a larger group (monster gets more HP). Shoot the monster: the bar should visibly shrink. A disguised monster should not be exposed by the bar.
7. **Sword:** Grab, aim and swing at the monster from about 1–3m away; check hit feedback while changing attack angles. Switch from blaster to sword and attack immediately.
8. **Blaster:** Aim at monster body and head; try repeatedly firing with the trigger held, then shoot a real wall and a leafy canopy. Real walls should block shots; leaves alone should not.
9. **Existing systems:** Confirm TKDYP, HROHN, STOP, TUFF badge, Rocket boost, Camera Lab and the shared tongue cooldown still work.

If the movement still jitters on only one map, send a short Quest recording with the map name and whether it happens near a particular tree/shack/wall. A desktop test cannot fully reproduce tracking issues.
