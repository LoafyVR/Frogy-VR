# Quest Beta Test — Frogy 17.2

1. Stand with both hands still; you should NOT drift or launch from a delayed impulse.
2. Plant a hand, push forward, stop that hand, then raise it. You should not receive a second, delayed speed burst. After release on the ground, motion should settle quickly.
3. Plant a hand and push **backwards and downwards together**. You should go forwards AND upwards in the same push, without pressing A.
4. Try a small downward ground push vs pressing A: A should still launch higher.
5. After jumping, swing both hands through empty air: no further push.
6. Push off a wall using a diagonal hand stroke. Verify both forward/lateral movement and upward boost work.
7. Pull the Quest **grip**: the finger formerly mapped to trigger curls. Pull **trigger**: the finger formerly mapped to grip curls. Thumb buttons unchanged.
8. Check weapon pickup, grappling, and camera menu still work.

If a fling happens, note whether the player was grounded or airborne and whether it came after a hand retracted, rested, or lost tracking. A screen recording is especially helpful.
