# Frogy 18 — Quest playtest

1. **Approved movement**: compare to 17.2 — push forward and up, short strokes, jump, and release. No change should be noticeable.
2. **Grounding**: stand and plant each paw; look at the contact from your headset, a friend's headset, and a follow/selfie recording. Paw tips should touch the floor without hovering. Rotate controllers and curl fingers while planted.
3. **Feet**: another player standing still should not hover when turning or looking around.
4. **Arms and hands**: arms remain connected, with no blocked view or floating fingertip balls. Both left/right palms mirror correctly.
5. **Sword**: grip a sword, tilt wrist, verify handle lies in hand and the blade projects outward. Drop and pick up again.
6. **Monster**: 2x-ish movement advantage, blue electric shot visible, unobstructed survivor stunned for about 3 seconds, wait for 9-second cooldown.
7. **Disguise**: survivor sees monster as a frog during disguise; attacking removes the disguise.
8. **Rocket**: activate boost, hold A to rise, release to descend, press A again before 16 seconds expire to climb again.
9. **Camera Lab**: open X -> CAMERA; test POV and third person. Test selfie-phone left grip to grab, release to park, recording button to capture. Try a short MIC ON recording with permission.
10. **Voice/mouth**: two Quests in one room; talk and see whether remote mouth opens and voice is audible. Check selfie camera mouth separately.
11. **Existing features**: medkits, sword pickup, privacy codes, voice, Players Online, maps, saving.

Report whether the hand floats, sinks into surfaces, or snaps when floor contact begins. Record a brief Quest clip if any do.
