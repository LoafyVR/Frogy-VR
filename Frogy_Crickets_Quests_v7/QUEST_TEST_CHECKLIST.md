# FROGY v6.4 — Quick headset test

- [ ] Launch https://frogy-vr.onrender.com in Quest Browser and select **ENTER FROGY VR**.
- [ ] Auto-join places you in the same public lobby as another player when there is room.
- [ ] Right and left hands appear at the same height as their respective physical controllers; there is ONLY ONE left and ONE right hand (no extra paws or green ground discs).
- [ ] Pull one hand backward while near the floor; your frog moves forward, without crouching.
- [ ] Lift a hand, move it forward, plant again and repeat; the body should not get dragged backward.
- [ ] Tap **left X**. Menu opens and STAYS OPEN without holding X.
- [ ] Tap **left X** again. Menu closes. All Settings buttons respond to a trigger press.
- [ ] Press **right A** to jump, and jump while already moving to preserve speed. Try tapping A just before landing to test jump buffering.
- [ ] Every player sees their own **YOU ARE THE MONSTER** or **YOU ARE A SURVIVOR** bubble when a round starts. At the end, everyone sees the winning players named in a bubble.
- [ ] Another player in your room can see your whole frog and BOTH hands.
- [ ] In the lobby's POND menu, choose **SERVER HOP**; the five-character public room code changes.

If anything fails, record the Quest model, whether the menu's bottom status shows `LEFT MISSING`/`RIGHT MISSING` or `PAW CONTACT 0/2`, and whether it happens in the lobby, match, or both. Don't physically force your arms to touch the real floor; play standing in a clear, safe space.
