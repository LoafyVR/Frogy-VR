# Frogy v10 Quest playtest checklist

For Quest 2 / 3 / 3S. Use **Meta Quest Browser**, not a desktop. Confirm version **v10.1** appears on the launcher.

- [ ] Both visible paws appear exactly once and follow their physical controllers.
- [ ] Stand normally; push off the ground and walls to move without hand jitter.
- [ ] Reach an actual wall. Hold **one trigger**, stay attached for approximately 4.5 seconds, then fall automatically.
- [ ] Repeat with **both paws on a wall and both triggers held**. Attachment lasts longer, up to approximately 7.6 seconds from first contact.
- [ ] Release trigger(s) while attached; falling starts immediately once no paw is attached.
- [ ] Slide a held paw downward; frog climbs upward without teleporting.
- [ ] Press **right A** while attached; the frog detaches and jumps, retaining horizontal travel momentum.
- [ ] Check that a rapid tracking flicker doesn't shake the hands or create duplicate hand models.
- [ ] Left **X** toggles the menu, stays open until tapped again. The menu trigger still clicks controls.
- [ ] First-time **HELP** includes a wall-sticking explanation.
- [ ] Lobby looks like a dark swamp; computers, shops and 7-stage parkour are still present.
- [ ] Voting board stays at the same location, buttons still work and benches/rug do not block it.
- [ ] Enter `FROG`/`FREE` on eligible account and confirm animation + distinct chime. Reuse of each code fails.
- [ ] Authorized moderator can redeem `IHVDL` once and sees MOD over their name; an ordinary player cannot claim MOD by typing it.
- [ ] MOD and OWNER see **REPORT** and **KICK** separately. Kicking another ordinary player removes them from the current lobby; a kick doesn't let them rejoin that room for 15 minutes. Reporting alone doesn't remove them.
- [ ] Two people can join the same public room and see each other, hands, and nameplates.

If any wall test fails, record which Quest model, whether it was in lobby or match, whether the hand was physically touching the wall, and ideally 10 seconds of video. Server tests cannot reproduce headset tracking issues.
